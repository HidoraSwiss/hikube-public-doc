This patch release brings back the **VM Instances** page, which could fail to display since v1.4.2.

<!-- truncate -->

## Fixes

- **VM Instances** — Since v1.4.2, the **VM Instances** page could fail to display. It displays normally again. Your virtual machines were never affected: only the console page was at fault, and no action is needed on your side.
